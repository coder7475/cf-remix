## Why

The homepage jumps from the hero directly to skills, missing an opportunity to quickly communicate core expertise areas. A "What I Do" section provides visitors with an immediate understanding of service offerings, improving engagement and guiding them toward relevant portfolio pieces.

## What Changes

- Add new "What I Do" section after hero banner
- Display 3 cards: Backend Development, Frontend Development, Deployment & DevOps
- Desktop: 3-column grid layout
- Mobile: Single column with horizontal scroll or stacked cards
- Each card includes icon, title, brief description, and relevant skill tags
- Cards link to relevant portfolio projects or skills section

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `what-i-do-section`: Hero-adjacent section showcasing core service areas with responsive card layout

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

- **Code**: New component file, homepage layout modification
- **UX**: Additional section between hero and skills, clearer service communication
- **Performance**: Minimal impact with static content

## Non-goals

- Interactive animations beyond hover effects
- Dynamic content loading
- Multiple card layouts or configurations
- Backend data fetching for card content
